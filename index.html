Pool

Private Heated Saltwater Pool

Enjoy our private outdoor saltwater pool with beautiful views. Perfect for relaxing after a day at the beach.

Pool Hours & Operation

Open 24 hours (available seasonally)

  • Pool light: Turns on from 6:00 PM – 12:00 AM
  • Pool pump: Runs 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM – 6:00 AM for circulation, cleaning, and heating

Heating

The pool is heated. The heater is turned off from May through September.

Cleaning Schedule

The pool is typically cleaned and chemically balanced weekly on Thursdays.

If service is scheduled during your stay, it usually takes place on Wednesdays or Thursdays between 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM. The crew will only access the backyard and pool area — they will not enter the home.

Skimmers are available on the side of the house (near the pool pumps) if you notice any debris.

Important

For safety reasons, please do not touch or adjust any pool equipment. If you need assistance, contact us and we’ll be happy to help.

Occasional issues may arise. Please inform us right away and we will address them promptly. Pool issues do not qualify for refunds.

Power Outage

⚡ Power Outage Reminder

To help protect the air conditioner, water heater, refrigerator, and other appliances from power surges or electrical instability, please follow the instructions below during a power outage.

During a Power Outage

Turn OFF the MAIN BREAKER.

This helps protect the appliances from possible power surges or electrical instability when electricity is interrupted.

When Power Returns

1. Wait 3–5 minutes.
Allow the electricity to stabilize.

2. Turn ON the MAIN BREAKER.

Important Reminder

Some appliances, including the air conditioner, water heater, and refrigerator, may malfunction after a power outage.

Please make sure the MAIN BREAKER is turned OFF during an outage and only turn it back ON after waiting 3–5 minutes once power has returned.

Thank you for helping us protect the appliances and prevent inconvenience during power interruptions.
